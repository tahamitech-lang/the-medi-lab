What's Included in Premium Health?

The Premium Health Package is our most popular comprehensive health screening package. It includes all essential blood tests, organ function tests, and metabolic screening. Perfect for individuals who want a thorough health checkup without the imaging components.

📋 Complete Blood Tests Included

  • 🩸 Complete Blood Count (CBC) Rs. 800
  • 🧬 Thyroid Profile (TSH, T3, T4) Rs. 1,200
  • 🫀 Liver Function Tests Rs. 800
  • 🫘 Kidney Function Tests Rs. 700
  • 🍗 Lipid Profile (Cholesterol) Rs. 1,000

Why Choose Premium Health Package?

Key Benefits

  • Comprehensive Blood Work: Complete analysis of all vital organs
  • Disease Prevention: Early detection of health problems
  • Cost Effective: Better value than booking tests individually
  • Time Saving: Single appointment for multiple tests
  • Professional Analysis: Expert interpretation of results
  • Detailed Report: Comprehensive health assessment

Who Should Get This Package?

Recommended For

  • Annual routine health checkup
  • Age 20 and above
  • Family history of chronic diseases
  • Pre-existing health conditions
  • Sedentary lifestyle
  • High-stress occupation

Preparation Guidelines

Before Your Test

  • 8-10 hours fasting (for accurate blood work)
  • Avoid strenuous exercise 24 hours before
  • Stay hydrated with water only
  • Wear loose-sleeved clothing
  • Arrive 10-15 minutes early
  • Continue regular medications unless advised otherwise
